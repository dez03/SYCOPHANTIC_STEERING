What specifically did i observe?
I noticed with chatgpt, that it would often overly praise me or conflate my accomplishments. it would make bold generalizations about my abilities and anchored it on my one off simple not-that-complex project that i worked on, or thing that i accomplished. 

Why does this matter? What is the risk (if so) if a model does this at scale?
It can create noise. When a user is trying to do something great in multiple domains and aspects of their life, with the aid of an LLM, the user might talk about their past accomplishments, upload their resume, or give context of their professional work experience so the model can help the user answer a niche and personal slightly technical question on a job application. a good response that's personal to you requires context. When you are trying to figure out how to increase your productivity in something like your workout routine, you mention you deadlift 505 pounds for 2. then you talk about a coding project you built, then move onto working on an application to a research program. you want to show that you're a person with a good head on your shoulders. the LLM might say "you are an active person who trains hard 5x a week, plays D1A rugby. you can handle this".
